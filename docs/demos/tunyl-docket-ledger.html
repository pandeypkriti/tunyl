    How it fits the tools you already run

    In
    WhatsApp, email or this page

    The person on site sends the photo the way they already send photos. No app to install.

    Tunyl
    Reads, flags, keeps the ledger

    Every docket becomes a row with the fields to check marked. Running totals per material per job.

    Out
    Your spreadsheet, Xero, ApprovalMax

    The ledger leaves as CSV or bill lines in the shape your accounts team already imports.

    The paper docket stays the record. The ledger is the working copy that used to be typed in by hand.
